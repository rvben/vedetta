Face matching runs on this Vedetta server. Face crops, visual fingerprints, and names are not sent to a cloud recognition service.

People are identities Vedetta has learned. Each card groups face crops that look like the same person — rename a card to lock in their identity.

Unmatched faces are face crops Vedetta found but couldn't link to a known person. Click one to assign or create a new identity.

Unidentified appearances are person events with no face match — usually because the face wasn't visible. Identify them to start tracking new people.

Exclude stops an identity or appearance from being considered for recognition. Skip for now leaves it in the review queue.
