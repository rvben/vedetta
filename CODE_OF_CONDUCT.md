# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Vedetta is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive
community. Examples of positive participation include:

- showing empathy and patience;
- giving actionable, evidence-based feedback;
- welcoming people with different backgrounds and experience levels;
- respecting privacy, safety, and responsible disclosure;
- focusing disagreement on ideas and observable behavior.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or sexualized
language, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, publishing private
information, or encouraging unsafe or unlawful surveillance.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful to
the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented
privately, and applied consistently.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical. Good-faith reports must not result in retaliation.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the project.
